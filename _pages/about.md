As of June 2026, I am a research associate in the Psycho-/Neurolinguistics and Pragmatics group at the Institute of Cognitive Science, University of Osnabrück. Before I started this role, I completed my Master's degree in Cognitive Science at the University of Osnabrück, specializing in linguistics and neuroscience. 

In my future research, I aim to explore the cognitive mechanisms underlying the production and comprehension of indirect language use.
